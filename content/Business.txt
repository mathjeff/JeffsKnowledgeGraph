How can I start a business?

  Starting a successful business is difficult and risky.

  It probably requires these things, and potentially more:

  * An idea that benefits people
  * Knowing who will pay for it
  * Incorporating a business
  * Getting enough money to develop the idea
  * Assembling a team that can make the idea work
  * Preventing competitors from copying it

  You might also consider:
  * Joining a business accelerator

Some things benefit many people

  Examples:
  * Cleaning up public spaces

Deciding to spend money can be hard

  * Spending money requires authorizing a payment
  * Spending money requires estimating whether the purchase is worthwhile
    * This requires estimating whether making the purchase is better than buying nothing
    * This requires estimating whether buying something else would be better

 - People don't usually like to spend money

People don't usually like to spend money

  People only have a limited amount of money.

  If a person runs out of money, then that person doesn't have any more money to spend.

  People often try to avoid spending money so they have enough money to spend on more important things.

Ways to get funding for a new business

  * Savings
    * If you have money from having worked before, that can help you to pay to run a business initially
  * Customers
    * If you can find customers and give them something useful, they might be willing to pay for it
    * Offering to sell to customers also gives you the opportunity to receive feedback about what customers will pay for and what they won't
  * Grants
    * Sometimes governments or foundations will give money to companies to help them to accomplish a goal
    * Receiving these grants might require that you submit an application
    * You should also check that the grant doesn't have other requirements that might distract your business from its most important goals
  * Venture Capital
    * You might be able give some a fraction of your company to a [venture capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venture_capital) firm in exchange for money

 - Funding via venture capital

Funding via venture capital

  You might be able give some a fraction of your company to a [venture capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venture_capital) firm in exchange for money
  * This may require convincing the venture capitalist that your company has a good chance to make enough money to be worth the investment
  * This may require finding the right venture capitalist who is interested in your business and/or familiar with type of things your business may be doing
  * You probably don't want to wait until you have run out of money to negotiate with a venture capitalist
    * Then you might only receive unfavorable offers (giving a large amount of your company for a small amount of money)
  * Venture capitalists may also offer advice, including introductions to people you could collaborate with
  * You might find that it takes a long time between when you pitch your idea to a venture capitalist and that person decides to invest in your company

 - What is a business accelerator?

Accelerators often offer machines

  If several companies each need to use a certain expensive machine for a small amount of time, it's often more efficient for several companies to each share the same machine than for each company to buy their own.

  So, business accelerators often contain expensive equipment that resident companies can use.

What is it like to work at a startup?

  * Busy
    * A startup usually has a small number of people and a lot of things to do, so it often involves a lot of work from each person
  * Varied
    * A startup usually has a small number of people and a variety of things to do, so it often involves each person doing a larger number of different things than they would at a larger company where they can specialize
  * Inspiring
    * In a startup, a person's compensation is usually more closely tied to the success or failure of the business than it is in a larger business
    * A person often joins a startup due to being inspired by or hopeful about its mission
