# Information about health

About smoking

  [Smoking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoking) refers to burning a substance and inhaling the resulting fumes.

  Smoking [can cause](https://www.lung.org/quit-smoking/smoking-facts/health-effects/smoking) a variety of health problems, including lung cancer.

  Please don't smoke.

 - Disclaimer

About secondhand smoke

  [Secondhand smoke](https://scienceinsights.org/what-is-secondhand-smoke-definition-and-health-risks/) is smoke that comes from another person smoking

  Secondhand smoke [can also cause](https://www.lung.org/quit-smoking/smoking-facts/health-effects/secondhand-smoke) health problems, including lung cancer.

Avoiding secondhand smoke

  If someone near you is smoking, you may be able to reduce the amount that you inhale in these ways:

  * If you believe it is safe to talk to this person, you may be able to ask this person to stop smoking or smoke somewhere else
  * You could go somewhere else
  * You could put up a barrier between you and the smoke
  * You could ask someone for help
    * If smoking is not permitted in your location, perhaps you can ask an authority to help enforce this
    * Perhaps you could ask someone for suggestions

About food spoilage

  [Food spoilage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_spoilage) is the process where food becomes dangerous to eat.

  Food spoilage is caused by microbes.

  Food spoilage can be slowed by:
  * Refigerating the food
  * Cooking the food

  Food spoilage can be expedited by:
  * Adding microbes to the food
    * This can happen if someone eats touches the food
