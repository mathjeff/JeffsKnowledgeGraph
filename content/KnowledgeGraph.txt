# Information about this knowledge graph itself

What is Jeff's Knowledge Graph?

  Jeff's Knowledge Graph is:
  * a dependency graph of knowledge, where each node is a piece of knowledge
  * a user interface to display the part of this knowledge that you're interested in

  Different from a usual [knowledge graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_graph)
    * Most other knowledge graphs are organized such that each node is an entity

  You're reading it right now!

How do I find information in Jeff's Knowledge Graph?

  * If you know the name of what you're looking for, first try going to the Home page, typing it into the Search box, and pressing Enter.

      * If this gives too many results:

          * Try making your query more specific by adding more words

          * Trying going to a more specific subtopic and searching there instead

      * If this gives too few results:

          * Try making your query less specific

          * Try [opening an issue](https://github.com/mathjeff/JeffsKnowledgeGraph/issues/new)

  * If you don't know the name of what you're looking for, try clicking the buttons at the bottom to explore

How can I share information from Jeff's Knowledge Graph?

  If you want to share a specific piece of information from Jeff's Knowledge Graph with another person:

  1. You can get its permalink by clicking the "permalink" link at the bottom
  1. Your browser should navigate to a page that represents that node in the graph
  1. You can copy that url from your browser
  1. You can send that url to someone else
  1. When that other person clicks on that link, it should send that person to the same piece of information that you were looking at

Why might I want to look at Jeff's Knowledge Graph?

  Easily see prerequisite information
  * When you are reading something confusing, you can easily view onscreen all of the information that you need to know before understanding what you are reading. There are buttons at the bottom to jump to, list, or outline dependencies.

  Skip familiar information
  * Jeff's Knowledge Graph keeps track of what you're already familiar with
  * When expanding prerequisite information inline, there is a separate button for displaying only unfamiliar information

  Browse related information
  * Other information that depends on the current piece of information is displayed at the bottom too

  Curated
  * We try to make the information here be useful.
  * Information here tends to have a similar style: helpful and concise.

Why might I want to write in Jeff's Knowledge Graph?

  Easy for viewers to see dependencies
  * If you want to write about a simple topic but it requires a lot of prior knowledge to understand it, you don't need to explain the prior knowledge more than once
    * You can simply reference the prior knowledge as a dependency

  Easy for viewers to skip dependencies
  * If a reader is already familiar with some prerequisite information, it's easy for that person to skip re-reading that information

  Dependencies more easily up-to-date
  * If some prerequisite information changes in the future, it doesn't necessarily need to be updated more than once

  Recognition for your contribution
  * Whenever someone checks who contributed to Jeff's Knowledge Graph, that person may see your name
